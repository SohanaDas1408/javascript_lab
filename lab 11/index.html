Architecture Highlights

ES6 Modules & Timers in Action

  • ES6 Modules: Code split into timer.js, scheduler.js, ui.js, notifications.js, and storage.js with strict import/export.
  • setInterval(): 1,000ms loop updates the live clock, checks active course progress %, and powers the countdown flip units.
  • setTimeout(): Handles 5-minute pre-class warning alerts, modal animations, and auto-dismissing toast notifications.
  • clearInterval() & clearTimeout(): Clean disposal prevents timer drift and memory leaks during pause, reset, and modal events.
